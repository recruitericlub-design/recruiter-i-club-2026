STATUTORY BASES ART. 23 § 1

Article 23 Sovereign Exemption

Under Article 23 of the Law of Ukraine “On Mobilization Preparation and Mobilization”, compulsory conscription and military registration apply exclusively to citizens of Ukraine. Non-citizen foreign passport holders hold unconditional statutory exemption from territorial recruitment center (ТЦК та СП) mobilization summons.

Key Legislative Excerpt:
“...foreigners and stateless persons legally residing in Ukraine are not subject to military conscription during martial law periods...”
